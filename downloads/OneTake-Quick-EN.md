# OneTake TestFlight — Quick tester SOP

**Required order: one Free scan of the past year → buy Lite → start Max trial → buy Max.**

**Lite and Max purchases are FREE in TestFlight.** Prices may appear on the buttons or confirmation sheet. Open the OneTake build installed through TestFlight; these test purchases do not transfer to the App Store release. [Apple's guidance](https://developer.apple.com/in-app-purchase/)

- Open OneTake from TestFlight. Note the build number and your iPhone/iOS version.
- Allow photo access. Full Access covers the library; limited access covers only selected photos.
- **Start on Free:** choose **Scan range → Last year** and **Match → Normal**. Tap **Scan Photos** once, wait for completion, and review the results before any purchase or trial.
- Open a set. Tap thumbnails to browse; use **Zoom in** to compare. **Tapping the large photo switches keep/delete.** Checkmark = keep; trash icon = delete.
- Test retaining a set with **Keep all → Next**, or defer it with **Skip**. For deletion, use unwanted test photos, check **Delete [number]**, then confirm Apple's prompt. Check the intended photos remain.
- **1 — After the Free scan, buy Lite, FREE in TestFlight:** Open **Upgrade / trial**, scroll to Lite, tap **Get Lite**, and finish the test purchase. Check **Lite is unlocked.**, Lite **Owned**, and the Lite allowance on Home. Try **All photos** and restoring skipped sets.
- **2 — Start the Max trial:** Open **Upgrade / trial → Start free trial**. Check Home shows **Max** with **trial until [date]**. Try **Custom dates**, smart-pick review and Mega clean. Review the batch before confirming deletion.
- **3 — Buy Max, FREE in TestFlight:** Reopen **Upgrade / trial → Get Max** and finish the test purchase. You do not need to wait three days. Check **Max is unlocked.** and Max **Owned**; the Max label alone does not prove purchase because the trial already shows Max.
- Close and reopen OneTake; check Max remains owned. Test **Restore purchases** and check it reports Max.
- Send feedback through TestFlight: build, phone/iOS, step, expected result, actual result and screenshot/video where useful. If trial eligibility or purchases block the sequence, report it; reinstalling does not reset the trial.

**Pass:** One Free scan with Last year selected completed → Lite ownership confirmed → dated Max trial confirmed → Max ownership and restore confirmed; keep/delete selections behaved as expected.
