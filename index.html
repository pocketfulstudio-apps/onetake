OneTake

Keep every moment, lose the nine extra takes.

OneTake finds similar photos on your iPhone so you can keep the best and clear the rest. Everything happens on your phone, with no accounts and no uploads.

Why people trust it
  • Pay once, no subscription. Free to start; Lite and Max are one-time purchases. No ads on any tier.
  • Nothing is deleted without you. Every delete goes through Apple's own prompt, and photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days.
  • See why each photo was picked. Every set says in one line why it suggests that photo, and you can keep any other.
