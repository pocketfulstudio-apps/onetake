OneTake Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

OneTake is run by Pocketful Studio. OneTake doesn't collect, store or share any of your data.

Your photos

OneTake needs access to your photo library to find similar photos. Photos are analysed only on your iPhone. They're never sent to us, because we have no servers.

If a photo is stored in iCloud, iOS may download it from Apple to your phone so it can be analysed. That happens between your phone and Apple.

You choose whether to allow all photos or only selected ones, and you can change this at any time in iPhone Settings.

Deleting photos

Photos are only deleted after you confirm in Apple's system prompt. They go to Recently Deleted, where iOS keeps them for 30 days.

No accounts, ads or tracking

OneTake has no accounts, advertising, tracking or analytics.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app which purchases you own so it can unlock them.

Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in iPhone Settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports. You can turn this off at any time.

Feedback and email

If you email us or send feedback, it goes through your own email app, and we only receive what you choose to send. We use it to reply and improve the app.

Data stored on your phone

Your settings, daily usage counts, skipped sets and freed-space total stay on your phone. Deleting the app removes them. The free trial's start date is kept in your phone's secure storage so the trial can only be used once.

Children

OneTake doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
