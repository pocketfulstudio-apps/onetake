Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated 1 November 2026. OneTake is run by Pocketful Studio.

In short
  • Your photos are analysed on your iPhone and never uploaded.
  • No accounts, ads, tracking or analytics.
  • Nothing is deleted until you confirm in Apple's prompt.
  • The app sends us nothing. We only hear from you if you email us.
  • Apple handles all payments.

1.Your photos

OneTake needs access to your photo library to find similar photos. Photos are analysed only on your iPhone, using Apple's built-in tools. They're never sent to us, because we have no servers.

If a photo is stored in iCloud, iOS may download it from Apple to your phone so it can be analysed. That happens between your phone and Apple.

You choose whether to allow all photos or only selected ones, and you can change this at any time in iPhone Settings.

2.Deleting photos

Photos are only deleted after you confirm in Apple's system prompt. They go to Recently Deleted, where iOS keeps them for 30 days.

3.No accounts, ads or tracking

OneTake has no accounts, advertising, tracking or analytics.

4.Third-party services

None. OneTake contains no third-party code or SDKs. It only uses Apple's own frameworks, including Photos, Vision and StoreKit.

5.Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app which purchases you own so it can unlock them.

6.Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in iPhone Settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports. You can turn this off at any time.

7.Feedback and email

If you email us or send feedback, it goes through your own email app, and we only receive what you choose to send. We use it to reply and improve the app. Ask us and we'll delete your emails.

8.TestFlight testers

If you test OneTake through TestFlight, Apple shows us your name and email, your device model and iOS version, and how many sessions and crashes you've had. It also passes on any feedback or screenshots you send. We only use these to run the test.

9.Data stored on your phone

Your settings, daily usage counts, skipped sets, purchase level and freed-space total stay on your phone. The widget reads your scan count and tier from there. Deleting the app removes them.

The free trial's start date is kept in your phone's secure storage so the trial can only be used once. It stays there after you delete the app.

The daily reminder is scheduled on your phone, and only if you allow notifications. The share card shows only the space you freed and the number of photos, and it only leaves your phone if you share it.

10.Your choices

  • Change or remove photo access in iPhone Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Photos.
  • Turn off the daily reminder in iPhone Settings, under Notifications.
  • Delete the app to remove the data listed above, apart from the trial date.

We don't hold your photos or an account for you, so there's nothing on our side to export or delete, apart from any emails you've sent us.

11.Children

OneTake doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

12.Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
