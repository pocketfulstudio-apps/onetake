OneTake Support

OneTake is run by Pocketful Studio. Need help? Email pocketfulstudio.apps@gmail.com. Tell us your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened.

My purchase isn't showing

Open the upgrade screen and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I get deleted photos back?

Yes, for 30 days. Open the Photos app, go to Recently Deleted, and recover them.

Why aren't all my photos included?

You may have allowed selected photos only. Tap Choose more photos in the app. To allow everything, go to iPhone Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Photos. Changing this makes iOS restart the app.

Do my photos get uploaded?

No. Photos are analysed only on your iPhone. See our privacy policy.
